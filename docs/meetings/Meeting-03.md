## Meeting Notes

### Task Management Guidelines

To ensure smooth workflow and clear accountability, we established the following rules:
- **Parallel Work:** Team members can work on multiple tasks simultaneously.
- **Prioritization:** We should tackle the more difficult tasks first, followed by the easier ones.
- **Task Ownership:** Every task has an owner. The owner is responsible for overseeing the task and reporting its status.
- **Reporting:** During meetings, the task owner must present:
    - What was completed.
    - What was not completed.
    - Where they got stuck (if applicable).
- **Representation:** The task owner represents themselves and their work. (It is better for the owner to present rather than the Project Manager).
